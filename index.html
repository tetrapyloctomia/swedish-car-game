Swedish Car Game

A word puzzle inspired by Swedish license plates. Make English words using 3 or 4 letters in the correct order. Choose Kid Mode or Grown-Up Mode.

Privacy Policy

Swedish Car Game works entirely offline and does not require an account.

The app does not collect or transmit personal information. Entered words are checked locally against the built-in dictionary.

Game statistics are stored locally on your device and are not sent to the developer.

The app does not use advertising or analytics services.
